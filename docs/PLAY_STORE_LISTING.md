## Short description

Islamic knowledge, Quran and books, prayer times, Qibla, and AI-assisted fatwa guidance.

## Full description

Fatwa App brings trusted Islamic knowledge and practical daily guidance into one calm, focused app.

### Explore Islamic knowledge

- Search Quran editions and selected Islamic books.
- Read Quran passages, Urdu and English translations, Hadith and Hanafi references.
- Browse fatwa answers with source references and scholar/institution details.
- Continue reading from the last saved place, including cached content for offline use.

### Ask Fatwa AI

Ask questions in natural language and receive retrieval-based guidance with references. AI responses are informational and should be confirmed with a qualified scholar for personal or complex matters.

### Prayer and Qibla

- View local prayer times in a compact 12-hour format.
- Set device-local Adhan alarms for Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib and Isha.
- Use prayer alarms after setup even when the internet is unavailable.
- Find the Qibla direction using the device location and compass.

### Community

- Read community reflections and Islamic reminders.
- Like, save and share posts.
- Upload text and optional images for review.
- View profiles, cover photos and community activity.

Fatwa App does not replace a qualified mufti or scholar. Please verify sensitive religious, legal or personal matters with a trusted local authority.

Confirm these answers against the final production configuration before submitting:

| Data category | Purpose | Optional? | Shared outside the app? |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Approximate location/city/country | Prayer times, Qibla, nearby feed and anonymous traffic analytics | Yes | Service providers / app backend |
| Name, email and profile details | Account and profile features | Yes | App backend |
| User questions and community posts | AI answers, moderation and community features | Yes | App backend and moderators |
| Photos | User avatar, cover and community post images | Yes | App backend/media storage |

| Push token | Device notifications | Yes | Firebase/notification service |
| App activity such as likes, saves and reading progress | Personalization and offline experience | Mostly local | App backend only where needed |

- Data is transmitted over HTTPS.
- Users can use the app without signing in for core reading features.
- Users can request account/data deletion through the in-app Data Deletion page or support email.
- Do not claim that all data is deleted automatically until the production deletion workflow has been tested end-to-end.
