Content & AI Disclaimer

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Educational use only

The AI assistant, the library and the community feed are for learning. They are not fatwas.

The AI can be wrong

The assistant answers from retrieved passages and general knowledge. It may misread a source, miss context, or be wrong on details of fiqh. Always check the cited source and ask a qualified mufti for personal rulings.

School of thought

Retrieved books lean on classical Hanafi texts, but the library also contains general sources (Quran, hadith collections). Not every passage has been reviewed by a scholar. Each text is labelled with its review status.

Translations

Urdu and English translations belong to their translators. Where rights are unconfirmed the app marks them “Translation rights unverified”.

Report a mistake

If you see an error in a text or an answer, tell us through the support email shown on the app store listing.
