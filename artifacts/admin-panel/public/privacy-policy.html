Fatwa App Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Fatwa App provides prayer times, Qibla guidance, multilingual Islamic content, questions and answers, notifications, and a moderated community post feature.

Information we use

When you grant permission, the app uses approximate device location while in use to calculate local prayer times, Qibla direction, and a location-based language suggestion. You can deny location access and continue using the app with a manually selected city.

The app stores a device identifier locally and uses it to sync notifications, submitted questions, answers, and post-review status. If you submit a question or community post, we process the name, gender selection, country, city, text, language preference, and any image you choose to attach. Uploaded community images are stored in Cloudflare R2 and are shown only after moderation approval.

How information is used

We use this information to provide app features, deliver answer and approval notifications, moderate community content, prevent duplicate or abusive submissions, and improve reliability. We do not currently use an advertising SDK or sell personal information.

Third-party services

The service uses Cloudflare Workers, D1, and R2 for the backend and media storage. The app may use Google Drive-hosted audio as the source for the optional adhan sound. These services process requests needed to deliver the features described above.

Retention and deletion

Approved community posts and their attached images may remain available as published content. Pending or rejected submissions are retained for moderation and audit purposes. You may request deletion of your profile-linked submissions or ask a moderator to remove a published post by contacting the app owner through the support contact shown in Google Play. Device-local data can be cleared by uninstalling the app or using the in-app profile deletion option.

Children and security

Fatwa App is not directed at children under 13. We use access controls, authenticated administration, and Cloudflare infrastructure, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact the Fatwa App owner through the support email listed on the Google Play store page.
