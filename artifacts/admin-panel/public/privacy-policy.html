Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Summary

We collect only what the app needs. We do not sell your data and we do not show advertising.

Information we use

Location (only if you allow it, and only while the app is open): to calculate prayer times and Qibla, to suggest your city and country, and to show nearby posts first. You can refuse and enter your city manually.

Profile: the name, country, city and optional picture you provide. Your name, picture and city are shown on posts you publish.

Posts and images you submit, and the questions you type to the AI assistant.

A random device identifier and a session token, so the app can remember your profile and sync notifications.

A push-notification token from Google Firebase Cloud Messaging, if you allow notifications, so we can send alerts about new posts and answers.

Basic technical logs (IP address, request time) created by our hosting provider for security and abuse prevention.

How we use it

To run the features above, to review posts, to send notifications you have allowed, to prevent abuse, and to keep the service reliable.

AI assistant

Your question is sent to an AI model hosted by Cloudflare Workers AI, together with passages found in the library, to produce an answer. Please do not put sensitive personal details in questions. Answers can be wrong; see the Content & AI Disclaimer.

Who processes data

Cloudflare (hosting, database, image storage, AI), Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (push delivery), and your device’s map/geocoding service for location names. These providers process data only to deliver the service.

Retention

Published posts, their images, likes and notification records are deleted automatically 30 days after publication or earlier. Rejected or pending submissions are kept briefly for moderation. Profile data is kept until you ask us to delete it.

Your choices and deletion

You can deny location or notification permissions in your device settings, turn off post alerts in the app, and request deletion of your profile and posts at any time. See the Data Deletion page.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Administration is restricted. No online service is perfectly secure.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests use the support email shown on the app store listing.
