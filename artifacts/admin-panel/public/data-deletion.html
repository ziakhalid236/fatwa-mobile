Data Deletion

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Delete your data

Send a deletion request using the support email shown on the app store listing, from the device or with the profile name you used. We delete your profile, picture, posts, likes, notifications and push token.

What is deleted automatically

Published posts and their images are removed 30 days after publication. Old notifications and rate-limit records are cleaned up daily.

On your device

Uninstalling the app removes locally stored settings, saved posts and alarms.
