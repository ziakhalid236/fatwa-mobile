Community Guidelines

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Be sincere and respectful

Share short reminders, verses, hadith or useful reflections. Cite your source when quoting.

Not allowed

Insults, hate or incitement against any person or group; takfir and sectarian attacks; unverified or fabricated hadith; political propaganda; advertising and spam; private information about others; sexual or violent content; copyrighted material you do not have the right to share.

Review process

Posts are reviewed by moderators before publishing. Published posts disappear after 30 days.

Reporting

To report a post, use our support email (contactfatwaapp@gmail.com). We aim to review reports promptly.
